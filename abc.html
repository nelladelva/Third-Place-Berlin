About Us

ABC is a community for Afro-descendant readers, writers, and professionals — built around books that speak to our histories, our present, and our future. What began as a single evening with Haitian poet James Noël has grown into an ongoing gathering: part reading circle, part professional network, part cultural home.

What We Do

Author Events

Readings, talks & signings with guest writers, in French, German, English, and Haitian Creole.

Community

Facilitated conversation that connects books to our own histories and lived experience.

Professional Network

A space for Afro-descendant professionals across fields to meet, collaborate, and support one another.

Who We're For

Anyone in the Afro-descendant community — and anyone who wants to read the Black world seriously — is welcome: students, academics, artists, founders, and professionals across every industry, based in Berlin and beyond.
