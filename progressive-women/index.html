About Us

Progressive Women Berlin is a space for women who care — about each other, about the city they're building a life in, and about the causes that shape that life. We meet to talk honestly, support one another, and turn conversation into real solidarity.

What We Do

Discussion Circles

Honest conversation on the issues that shape our lives — political, personal, and everything between.

Mutual Support

A network to lean on — advice, referrals, and real solidarity when you need it.

Advocacy & Action

Turning conversation into action, from local organizing to everyday allyship.

Who We're For

Progressive women of every background building a life in Berlin — organizers, professionals, students, and anyone who wants community that shares their values.
