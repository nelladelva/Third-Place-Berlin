Not home. Not work. Somewhere in between.

A space for multicultural professionals who just landed in Berlin — to connect, work alongside each other, trade ideas, and actually build a life here. Expats, introverts, and nerds welcome.

What This Is

Moving to a new city is easy to survive and hard to belong to. Third Place Berlin exists for the people in between — multicultural professionals figuring out where they fit, who want more than small talk at a networking mixer. It's a "third place": not your job, not your apartment, but the room where you actually meet people.

Here, that room holds a handful of communities — reading groups, co-working meetups, culture nights, volunteer projects — each one a way in.

What You'll Find Here

Professional Network

Meet multicultural professionals across industries — the connections that don't show up on LinkedIn.

Cultural Exchange

Conversations, readings, and gatherings rooted in the cultures we all carried here with us.

Co-Working

Low-key sessions to work alongside others — good for the days remote work feels a little too remote.

Volunteering

Ways to give back to Berlin, and to each other, beyond the usual expat bubble.

Learning Opportunities

Workshops, skill-shares, and talks to grow — professionally and personally.
