About Us

Belong Beyond Borders (BBB) is a comprehensive community for people who want more than one kind of connection. We gather around culture, around work, and around the things we simply enjoy doing together — built for people who've crossed a border or two to get here, and everyone who wants in.

What We Do

Cultural Events

Gatherings that celebrate and explore the traditions and cultures we all carry with us.

Co-Working

Regular sessions to sit down and work alongside people who get it.

Shared Hobbies

Interest-based meetups — games, sports, crafts, and whatever else brings people together.

Who We're For

Like-minded people looking for a well-rounded community — professionals, culture lovers, and hobbyists, newcomers to Berlin and longtime locals alike.
